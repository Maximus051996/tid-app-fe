Backup & sync

Move your data between devices

Export your tasks, goals, notes, and investments to a portable file, then import it on another device. The app stays frontend-only — your data never leaves your machines unless you choose to share the file.

How it works

  1. Export a backup file from the device with your latest data.
  2. Move the file to the other device (USB, email yourself, cloud drive — your choice).
  3. Import the file there. Choose Merge to combine, or Overwrite to replace.

Tip

Use Merge when you've been editing on both devices. It keeps the most recently updated version of each row. Use Overwrite only when one device is the authoritative source and the other should match it exactly.

Privacy

Backup files contain everything you've created in the app. Treat them like any other document — don't share them publicly. Passwords are stored as PBKDF2 hashes, not plaintext.
